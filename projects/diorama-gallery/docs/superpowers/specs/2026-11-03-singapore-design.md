# Singapore — Garden City
Intent: build the supplied miniature-map Singapore scene as a living second world in Small Worlds. User requested immediate construction and opted out of repeated optional approvals. Existing Three.js plus diorama-worlds is selected by builder to preserve launcher/offline/thumbnail integration; video and a new engine would lose interaction or duplicate infrastructure.

Use reference's warm map-paper base, glass and cream architecture, dense garden, small visitors and transport. Marina Bay Sands rear-left, gardens and Flyer rear-right, lotus left, Merlion and civic center, skyline/temple right, railway foreground and green bus. Deliberately stylized arrangement, not a navigation map. Dynamic water/fountain/boats/wheel/train, no sound. Original geometry and procedural textures only, no copying reference into rendered scene. Mobile readable, capped DPR and reusable materials, one active GPU scene. Preserve harbor files, no main promotion. A high craft target is not evidence of achieved AAA fidelity.
