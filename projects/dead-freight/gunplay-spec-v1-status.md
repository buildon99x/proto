## Coverage and boundaries

The named specification scenarios T01–T06, T19 and T21–T24 receive model/source coverage through `test-r4-foundation.cjs`, `test-loadout-action.cjs`, `test-muzzle-ballistics.cjs`, `test-input-router.cjs`, `test-inventory-game.cjs`, `test-stash-profile.cjs`, `test-audio-preparation.cjs`, `test-audio-readiness.cjs` and `test-gunplay-foundation.cjs`. These are not recordings of human play. A rendererless harness exercises the real Three.js/game/core modules with substituted DOM, renderer and audio devices.

The final complete suite run passed 720 checks in 32 suites, including physically held versus released auto-reload continuation, schema 4 backup namespace isolation, settings stale-reset protection and confirmed lost-acknowledgement recovery. Syntax, static packaging and metadata validation passed. The documented Node loader route ran all 11 unchanged project builders, then launcher compilation/type checks and all 27 pages successfully. Pinned pnpm 9.15.4 was restored using a workspace-local Corepack cache and an offline frozen install from the existing dependency store. The literal root commands still stop at the tsx CLI IPC socket (EPERM); the equivalent complete aggregate stages passed without changing repository dependencies, security or network settings. The input/audio review also found and corrected stale Start cancellation, late native lock grants, focus recovery scanner-induced switch cancellation, and modal cancellation after remapping pause. Randomized source-harness sequences conserved ammo for two R4 instances and R4/pistol pairs.

The following remain open: a complete P0 exit decision, P1 migration/polish of the other three guns, sniper/DMR, melee, crouch/cover, prepared throw and trajectory display, smoke/impact variants, robot part reactions, full interruption/scene restore, all six classes, two authored city maps, completed AI/economy, and five-player play evaluation. Existing grenades/scanners and single-region extraction are preserved, not relabeled as these future systems.

## Actual acceptance sequence

1. Use the explicit `?testProfile=local-qa` URL. Check its test-save badge before any inventory, deployment or currency action.
2. Verify the separately published 0.9 capacity/overflow checkpoint before changing its test baseline.
3. On 0.10, capture a cold menu: bank preparation, one Start, no pre-ready save/time change, exact lock diagnostic, pause/continue, focus cancel and retry.
4. Exercise R4 at 5/15/35/70/120 m; auto/burst, manual and empty reload, interruptions before/after commit, H, same-class swaps, close walls, airborne/sprint ADS, remapping and repeated inputs.
5. Record gameplay at 30/60 FPS, measure p95 input delay and 10-minute frame times, and listen to the real device output. Finish successful extraction, death and replay with inventory conservation.

At this checkpoint those live steps have not passed for 0.10. Prior Windows tests of 0.8.2–0.8.4 established partial movement/combat/retry behavior, while successful extraction, sound quality and ordinary relative-mouse control remained unverified. `WrongDocumentError` is the observed pointer-lock result, not proof of a permission denial or a detached canvas.
