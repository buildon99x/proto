Acceptance checks

  1. Does each slash lead naturally into a clearly stronger contact? Does the third strike feel heavier?
  2. Can you distinguish a body hit from metal armor without an electronic beep?
  3. Can you hear monster tells, hurt and dodge during Whirling Axes?
  4. Do War Cry and the environment fit a ruined fantasy world?
  5. Is anything sharp, clipped, monotonous, too loud or tiring after repeated combat?
  6. In real gameplay, do interrupted windups stop and misses avoid impact sounds?

Subjective listening and normal-control gameplay still need explicit review. This page makes those checks possible and does not pre-mark them as passed.
