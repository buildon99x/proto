## 3. Shared execution contract

### 3.1 Purchase, compatibility and preview

All Expert purchases require trained Expert tier, the complete starter set, known node ID, whole next rank, enough SP, existing exact spent-SP ledger, and the real trainer command path. E minimum parent rank stays1. No extra XP/SP, free Expert core, inferred rank or automatic purchase accompanies promotion. Max rank equals the catalog cost-column count; sum every acquired column exactly once. L15 grants5 SP through existing progression; one core costs3, two cores or core+Banner branch cost6. If earlier points were spent, no free refund or forced purchase solves that choice.

Register `battle_banner_support: ['banner_of_protection','banner_of_zeal']` in real purchase, preview and load validation. Keep `righteous_hammer_damage_type` unchanged. Every other Expert combination is **E-compatible**, subject to parent purchase, rather than “source-confirmed compatible”; do not add an unrecorded exclusivity. Cleansing+Turn Undead+Invigorating Touch coexist. Returning hammer works with either legal hammer branch. Lance+Shield coexist. No respec is promised until its own paid handler exists; use separate legal profiles to compare Banner branches.

Preview must show current→next rank, SP, resource per payment and maximum channel budget, true CD, radius/range/time units, parent/trainer/tier/branch blockers and authored-versus-reference values. A null source MP row should display “원작 MP 비용 미확인 / 현재 구현은 MP 소모 없음” only after the explicit E policy below is implemented. Missing binding, insufficient maximum MP/stamina and branch lock have different explanations. The purchase reducer must not refill pools or reset cooldowns.

### 3.2 Snapshot, live conditions and clock

Use an immutable **accepted action spec**: paid rank, parent/augment ranks, permanent attributes/gear, raw coefficients, costs, cast/attack speed, cast-origin offensive Banner/Inner Flame multipliers, critical chance/multiplier, range and shape. Never recompute these from a later paused purchase or equipment change. Projectiles, fire areas and the full Onslaught including its Lance inherit that spec. Cost is quoted from the same resolver used to charge; reject before mutation if any required number is unresolved/nonfinite. Do not use `sourceValue ?? 0` for unresolved mechanics.

Target HP, position, wall LOS, armor/armor-break, current resistance, Shackles and Punish eligibility are evaluated at each actual hit. Incoming Protection and Inner Flame resistance use the hero's live state at the incoming hit. Reactive Thorns has no paid cast; freeze its offensive/passive spec at that qualifying block event, after the active block payment, then use the same terminal-hit rules. All temporary effects expire before same-timestamp resolution; split a frame at expiry, payment, contact or channel-end deadlines where ordering matters. Cast speed affects startup/recovery/contact timing, not source cooldowns,15s Banner lifetime,3s Inner Flame duration,4s channel window or effect ticks.

Use active simulation seconds consistently with the existing hitstop clock, no wall-clock/offline catch-up. Pause/blur/hidden tab/store-busy/arrival wait accrue no damage, regen, durations or cooldown time. An accepted damaging action breaks arrival safety; Banner/self-heal alone does not, so neither can be precast while the world remains indefinitely frozen. Repeated input at dt0 must never tick a resource, proc or payload twice.

Banner membership is a pure query of current hero position, same region, age and LOS. Query at action acceptance and incoming-hit resolution, after applicable movement/expiry, rather than retaining a stale `insideBanner` flag. Leaving the ring removes live defense/cast eligibility immediately; an already paid outgoing spec stays frozen. Do not refresh a persistent projectile from an aura every frame.

### 3.3 Damage and event identity, including recursion guards

Introduce a bounded internal hit context, not user-controlled command fields: `castId`, `hitId`, `originSkillId`, `rootKind`, `procDepth`, `criticalEligible`, `canTriggerOnHit`, `attackerId`, `attackFamily`, and component IDs for mixed damage. A cast ID is monotonic within a battle; a hit ID identifies its target+leg/tick/contact. Never use `b.time` alone as unique identity, and never append every hit to permanent `claimedRewards`.

E damage pipeline:

1. Existing action coefficient and existing STR/INT/armor/weapon rules form the raw component. The permanent physical multiplier is already present in today's mainhand basis; apply it exactly once, including Lance/Guilt raw physical portions. Fire/pure never receive armor penetration or the permanent physical-only multiplier.
2. Multiply outgoing raw components by snapshotted Banner damage bonus, then Inner Flame for fire only. No damage buff modifies healing. Inner Flame's source increase is a percentage modifier, not extra flat damage.
3. One critical result per eligible root hit, shared across a mixed packet's components; never one roll per damage type. Use current target defenses, then live Shackles and Punish multipliers exactly once. Pure bypasses armor and elemental resistance, but retains explicitly listed outgoing/target amplification.
4. Subtract actual HP once for the aggregated hit, cap effective dealt damage to pre-hit HP, emit one hit/defeat result, then post-hit status/procs. Do not allow component1 killing a target to drop component2 bookkeeping or duplicate defeat/XP.

E critical eligibility: mace keeps existing weapon criticals. Direct hammer hits, direct Judgement, each paid Onslaught damage pulse, its end Lance and Turn Undead are spell-root hits; use base `criticalChance` plus Flaming Wrath bonus (clamped0..1), with the current1.5 base critical multiplier. Trial by Fire ticks, Wrath lightning ticks, Flames of Devotion, Thorns, Flaming Wrath explosions and Guilt splashes **do not crit or generate spell-critical procs**. This is explicit authored mapping, not a wiki claim. Adding spell criticals changes earlier spell damage; include Apprentice/Adept regression and play calibration, not just an Expert-only fixture.

Secondary damage (`procDepth:1`) may kill and settle its victim normally, but **cannot** emit another spell cast, Flaming Wrath, Guilt, Lawbringer, Thorns or a secondary-on-secondary event. Area auras emit no attack at all. `hitEnemy` should return a structured result; downstream skills must not infer a real hit from floater/sound or call `hurtHero` to model reflection. No cross-owner reflected damage loop; future enemy reflect must retain the same terminal origin policy.

Lawbringer draws once per eligible incoming attack, even when both block and positive damage occurred. One projectile/area attack needs an authoritative source identity; null/dead/off-map attackers, environment damage, already-invulnerable ignored hits and terminal reflection cannot trigger it. Thorns remains only successful melee block against that attacker, samples post-active-block-payment stamina, and does not fire on ranged shots. A successful passive physical block still pays no active6-stamina charge under the existing policy.

Flaming Wrath/Guilt each have one **caster-wide** effect CD, not one per target, projectile, slot or component. On a multi-hit simultaneous event, stable `(event time, cast sequence, target ID)` order claims an available proc once. Set the CD before emitting secondary damage. Persist activated CDs in the same critical checkpoint so reload cannot reset them.

The current battle always recreates `seed:1369781` and shares RNG with cosmetic death particles. For the new critical/Lawbringer path, separate combat RNG from FX RNG and persist its unsigned32-bit state plus safe-integer draw count in strict recovery. A successful random combat outcome checkpoints the advanced state, even when a proc fails its chance roll; visual density/flashes must not change chances. Rest/retry/travel retain that RNG record rather than reseed it. Old versions initialize the new record only through explicit migration (E initial state1369781, draw count0), with no offline rolls. This prevents ordinary reconnect from restarting a known favorable sequence; it is not an anti-tamper claim.
