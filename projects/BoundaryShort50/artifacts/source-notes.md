# Evidence and limits

The script was supplied after independent source verification. The original unsupported brain-damage/brain-region-overload/energy-vampire causation is excluded.

- Givi & Kirk (2024): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38079463/ . Scope: declining invitations to enjoyable social activities and overestimating negative inviter reactions. Do not generalize to workplaces, abusive/coercive relationships, or every relationship. The giant thought bubble versus smaller reply is an explicitly labeled qualitative visual metaphor, not a fake measured graph or a guarantee of acceptance.
- Centre for Clinical Interventions, Assert Yourself Module6: https://www.cci.health.wa.gov.au/~/media/CCI/Consumer-Modules/Assert-Yourself/Assert-Yourself---06---How-to-Say-No-Assertively.pdf . Brief, honest refusal and calmly repeating a short response. The ten-minute offer is optional only if willing and able.
- Mayo Clinic: https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/stress-management/in-depth/assertive/art-20044644 . General assertive communication guidance.

“일정 보고 답할게” is a concrete practice example, not a sentence with demonstrated clinical efficacy. In relationships involving threats/retaliation, safety and assistance take priority over refusal technique. No diagnosis, universal safety claim, algorithm or viral guarantee is presented.

Visuals are original project SVG/GSAP artwork. No external stock imagery, famous characters, doctor imagery, medical scans or cloned person voice. Local Korean voice preparation uses public MIT MeloTTS code/model documentation; model downloads are blocked (see voice-blocker.json), so the current visual preview is not a completed narrated video.
